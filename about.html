What We Stand For

Mission, Vision & Motto

Grounded in the Seventh-day Adventist calling, everything we do flows from these foundational commitments.

🌍
Our Mission

Make Disciples of Jesus Christ

To make disciples of Jesus Christ who live as His loving witnesses and proclaim to all people the everlasting gospel of the Three Angels' Messages in preparation for His soon return (Matt 28:18–20, Acts 1:8, Rev 14:6–12).

✨
Our Vision

Restoration of All Creation

In harmony with Bible revelation, we see as the climax of God's plan the restoration of all His creation to full harmony with His perfect will and righteousness — a world made new.

💛
Our Slogan

Freedom, Healing & Hope

Seventh-day Adventists are devoted to helping people understand the Bible to find freedom, healing, and hope in Jesus. This is the heartbeat of every ministry we run.

Our Motto

"The Advent Message to All the World in My Generation."

This timeless SDA motto captures our urgency, our scope, and our personal responsibility in sharing the Gospel before Christ's soon return.

What Guides Us

Core Values

Guided by the Bible and the Holy Spirit, these Christ-centred values shape every aspect of our ministry life.

01

Christ-Like Living

Illustrating the lordship of Jesus in our lives through moral, ethical, and social behaviours consistent with His teachings and example.

02

Christ-Like Healing

Affirming the biblical principles of whole-person wellbeing — we make healthful living and healing of the sick a priority through compassionate ministry.

03

Christ-Like Serving

Following Jesus' example, we commit to humble service — ministering to individuals most affected by poverty, tragedy, hopelessness, and disease.

04

Scripture-First

We are called by God and guided by the Bible. Every decision, programme, and relationship is measured against the authority of God's Word.

05

Witnessing & Service

Witnessing and serving others represents the character of Jesus Christ. Everyone has God-given gifts and the responsibility to use them actively.

06

True Worship

Genuine worship of our Creator occurs through fellowship, prayer, singing, and studying the Bible together as a Spirit-filled community.

07

Community & Belonging

Everyone has a place in God's family. We build an environment of authentic relationships where every person is known, loved, and supported.

08

Integrity & Excellence

We pursue transparency and accountability in all things — and give God our very best in worship, leadership, service, and stewardship.

09

Empowered by the Spirit

The Holy Spirit transforms lives and equips believers with abilities to advance God's kingdom. We operate in dependence on His power daily.

Our Journey

A History of Faithfulness

2013

Founded in a Living Room

Growing in Christ Ministry began with a small prayer group of 12 people meeting weekly in Nairobi, Kenya, with a vision to disciple the next generation.

2016

First Permanent Home

The congregation moved into its first dedicated facility and launched the Youth Ministry and Community Outreach programmes.

2019

Annual Conference Launched

The first annual Growing in Christ conference drew 500 attendees from across Kenya, centred on the Three Angels' Messages and discipleship.

2022

Expansion & New Ministries

Launched Children's Ministry, Mentorship Programme, and began weekly community health outreaches across Nairobi.

2025

15,000 Lives Impacted

A milestone year — the ministry reached 15,000 lives through outreach, discipleship, and conference programmes across Kenya.
