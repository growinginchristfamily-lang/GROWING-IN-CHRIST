About Ellen G. White

The Most Translated American Author

Ellen G. White (1827–1915) wrote more than 100,000 pages on spiritual and practical topics over a 70-year ministry. Guided by the Holy Spirit, her works have been published in over 160 languages and remain an authoritative source of truth for the Seventh-day Adventist Church.
