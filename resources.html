28 Fundamental Beliefs

The 28 Fundamental Beliefs represent the core doctrinal positions of the Seventh-day Adventist Church — grounded entirely in Scripture and held by Growing in Christ Ministry.

From the nature of God and salvation by grace, to the Sabbath, the Second Coming, and healthy living — these beliefs guide our worship, our community, and our mission.

Inspired Writings

Ellen G. White

Ellen G. White (1827–1915) was a co-founder of the Seventh-day Adventist Church and a prolific Christian author. Her writings are considered an expression of the spirit of prophecy and are a continuing source of truth and guidance.

Browse and download her most widely read books, including Steps to Christ, The Great Controversy, The Desire of Ages, and many more — all available free of charge.
