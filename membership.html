Why Join?

Benefits of Membership

Membership is not just a title — it's a commitment to belong, grow, serve, and be accountable within a community of faith.

A Spiritual Home

Find belonging in a community that knows your name, walks with you, and celebrates your growth.

Discipleship Access

Priority access to Bible training, small groups, and discipleship programmes designed for members.

Ministry Involvement

Discover and deploy your spiritual gifts by serving in one of our active ministries.

Pastoral Care

Receive dedicated pastoral support, counselling, and prayer from our leadership team.

Network & Fellowship

Connect with a wide network of believers across Nairobi and the broader Growing in Christ community.

Leadership Pipeline

Be identified for mentorship and developed for leadership within the church and beyond.

The Process

How to Become a Member

Membership at Growing in Christ Ministry is a thoughtful, intentional process designed to ensure every member is equipped and connected.

1

Attend Consistently

Begin attending our Sunday services and midweek programmes regularly to experience our community.

2

Complete the New Believers Class

A 4-week foundational class covering salvation, baptism, the Holy Spirit, and church life.

3

Baptism

Water baptism by immersion as a public declaration of faith in Jesus Christ.

4

Submit Your Membership Form

Complete the membership application below and meet with a pastor for a brief welcome conversation.

5

Welcome & Commissioning

Be officially welcomed into the church family during a Sunday service and connected to a small group.
