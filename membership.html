The Process

How to Become a Member

Membership at Growing in Christ Ministry is a thoughtful, intentional process designed to ensure every member is equipped and connected.

1

Attend Consistently

Begin attending our Sunday services and midweek programmes regularly to experience our community.

2

Complete the New Believers Class

A 4-week foundational class covering salvation, baptism, the Holy Spirit, and church life.

3

Baptism

Water baptism by immersion as a public declaration of faith in Jesus Christ.

4

Submit Your Membership Form

Complete the membership application below and meet with a pastor for a brief welcome conversation.

5

Welcome & Commissioning

Be officially welcomed into the church family during a Sunday service and connected to a small group.
