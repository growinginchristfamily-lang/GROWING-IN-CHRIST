Worship & Prayer

Our worship ministry exists to usher the congregation into the presence of God through Spirit-led, Word-anchored worship. We believe that genuine worship is the foundation of all we do.

Our prayer ministry intercedes for our congregation, the nation of Kenya, and the nations of the world — meeting weekly and in special prayer gatherings throughout the year.
