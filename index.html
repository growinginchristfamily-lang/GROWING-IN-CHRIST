Who We Are

A Church Built on the Word of God

Growing in Christ Ministry is a faith community in Nairobi, Kenya, devoted to making disciples, building families, and transforming communities through the power of the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

We gather to worship, grow in Scripture, serve the needy, and equip every believer to live out their calling — whether in the home, workplace, or mission field.

"But grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. To him be glory both now and forever!"
— 2 Peter 3:18 (NIV)

Our Foundation

What Drives Everything We Do

Our Mission

To make disciples of Jesus Christ who live as His loving witnesses — growing in faith, serving in love, and sharing the hope of the Gospel with all of Kenya and beyond.

Our Vision

A spiritually mature, outward-looking church that raises transformed believers who impact every sphere of society with the grace and truth of Jesus Christ.

Our Values

Scripture-anchored faith, Spirit-led worship, servant-hearted leadership, radical generosity, and a deep love for every person God places in our path.
