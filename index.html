Who We Are

A Church Built on the Word of God

Growing in Christ Ministry is a faith community in Nairobi, Kenya, devoted to making disciples, building families, and transforming communities through the power of the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

We gather to worship, grow in Scripture, serve the needy, and equip every believer to live out their calling — whether in the home, workplace, or mission field.

"But grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. To him be glory both now and forever!"
— 2 Peter 3:18 (NIV)
